Part 1: Website Research Assignment Introduction This research investigates four eco-focused websites — GreenSprout, EarthHero, Ecoternatives, and Vintage Green Review. The analysis examines how each website operates, why it is effective, how its layout is structured, and why the chosen layouts support user experience. The study also identifies common patterns across the websites, linking them to the broader advantages of having an online presence for sustainable businesses.

GreenSprout (Eco Baby Products) How it works: GreenSprout functions as a specialised e-commerce site for sustainable baby products, including bibs, bottles, teethers, and diapers. Products are categorised clearly for parents, making navigation straightforward. The site also displays trust signals such as eco-certifications, “1% for the Planet” badges, and sustainability commitments. Why it works: The site builds trust by addressing a very specific need — safe, non-toxic baby products. Parents value transparency and safety, which is emphasised in both product descriptions and branding. Layout: The layout is clean and product-focused, with neutral backgrounds that make items stand out. A top navigation bar highlights product categories, the shopping cart, and the brand story. Why the layout works: The simple structure reduces friction for busy parents and maintains consistent branding through a palette of greens, whites, and soft tones. This reinforces both eco-values and product safety.
